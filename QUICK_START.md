# 🚀 QUICK START GUIDE

## Getting Started in 3 Minutes

### 1. Download & Extract
```
M4NFROID_GALACTICA_v3_0_0_GITHUB_READY.zip
```
Extract to any folder on your computer.

### 2. Open & Play
Double-click **`index.html`** in the extracted folder.

**Supported Browsers**:
- ✅ Chrome / Chromium (Recommended)
- ✅ Firefox
- ✅ Edge
- ✅ Safari (macOS)

### 3. Controls

| Action | Key/Mouse |
|--------|-----------|
| Move | WASD or Arrow Keys |
| Aim | Mouse |
| Fire | Space or Left Click |
| Interact | E |
| Pause Menu | ESC |

### 4. First Run Tutorial

1. **Hub Screen**
   - Click "Act 1" to start
   - Click "START RUN" button

2. **In Combat**
   - Move with WASD
   - Mouse aims automatically
   - Space to fire
   - Kill enemies → collect loot (purple drops)
   - Stand on portal → press E to next zone

3. **Managing Inventory** (Press ESC anytime)
   - **Inventory Tab**: See all items
     - Use filter buttons: ALL / WEAPON / SHIELD etc.
     - Click "⚔️ Equip" to equip an item
     - Click "💰 Sell" to sell for scrap
   
   - **Equipment Tab**: See what's equipped
     - Click "↩️ Unequip" to move back to stash
   
   - **Stats Tab**: View your character stats
   
   - **Skills Tab**: (Coming soon!)

4. **Progression**
   - Kill enemies → Gain XP → Level up
   - Better items drop at higher depths
   - Rare/Epic/Legendary items are powerful
   - Boss every 10 zones (guaranteed rare+ drop!)

### 5. Tips for Beginners

✅ **Early Game (Level 1-25)**
- You get bonus drop rates!
- Common/Uncommon/Rare items drop frequently
- Equip the best items you find
- Don't worry about hoarding - sell bad items

✅ **Equipment Strategy**
- Weapon: Highest damage
- Shield: More survivability
- Engine: Movement speed
- Reactor: Energy (coming soon)

✅ **When to Use Each Act**

| Act | Your Level | Difficulty | Best For |
|-----|------------|------------|----------|
| Act 1 | 1-100 | Easy | Learning, farming |
| Act 2 | 100-250 | Medium | Better loot |
| Act 3 | 250+ | Hard | Endgame |
| Act 4 | Any | Dynamic | Challenge |

### 6. Save System

Your progress saves **automatically** to your browser's localStorage:
- Items in stash
- Equipped items
- Character level & XP
- Act unlocks
- Resources (scrap, cells)

**To manually save**: Press ESC → Click "💾 Save"

**To reset**: Clear browser data or delete localStorage

### 7. Troubleshooting

#### Game won't load?
- Use a modern browser (Chrome recommended)
- Check browser console (F12) for errors
- Disable browser extensions

#### Items not dropping?
- Make sure you're killing enemies (not just avoiding)
- Early game boost ends at level 25
- Check drop rate: Base 12% + boost

#### Can't equip items?
- Press ESC to open pause menu
- Go to Inventory tab
- Click "⚔️ Equip" button on item
- Check Equipment tab to confirm

#### Game too hard?
- Play Act 1 (easiest)
- Equip better items from stash
- Farm earlier zones for better gear
- Level up for stat bonuses

#### Game too easy?
- Progress to later acts
- Try Act 4 (scales with you!)
- Push higher depths
- Look for Epic/Legendary items

### 9. Advanced Tips

🎯 **Loot Optimization**
- Don't pick up everything - be selective
- Use slot filters to find upgrades fast
- Sell Common items for scrap
- Keep best Rare+ in stash

🎯 **Build Planning**
- Focus on either HP or Damage early
- Crit builds need Crit Chance + Crit Damage
- Fire Rate builds need Fire Rate + Damage
- Shield builds need Shield + Regen

🎯 **Progression Path**
```
Level 1-25:  Act 1, farm items, learn mechanics
Level 25-50: Act 1, push depth, find Rares
Level 50-100: Act 1/2, optimize build
Level 100+:  Act 2/3, Epic+ farming
Level 250+:  Act 3/4, endgame
```
